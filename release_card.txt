## Breaking Changes

- Requires dnd5e 6.1.0 as both minimum and verified version.

## New Features

- Day breakdowns and time advance use the traveller's own hours per travel day.
- Groups travel at their primary vehicle's speed and appear in the mount configuration menu.
- Groups with a slowed member are held to slow pace.
- Pace selector is disabled for vehicles that do not travel on land.
- Travel By selector picks between multiple land, water or air speeds and defaults to the fastest.
