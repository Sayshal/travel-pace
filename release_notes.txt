## Breaking Changes

- Requires dnd5e 6.1.0 as both minimum and verified version.

## New Features

- Day breakdowns and time advance use the traveller's own hours per travel day.
- Groups appear in the mount configuration menu.
- Groups travel at their primary vehicle's speed.
- Groups with a slowed member are held to slow pace, and the chat card shows a slowed note.
- Pace selector is disabled with a note for vehicles that do not travel on land.
- Travel By selector for travellers that can travel by more than one of land, water or air. Defaults to the fastest.

## Bug Fixes

- Advance time no longer drops whole days in Calendaria worlds.
- Notifications show localized text instead of raw keys.
- Saving the mount configuration menu no longer clears already enabled mounts.
- The advance-time confirmation shows a localized duration.
- Travel speeds the system derives from hidden movement types such as jump are ignored.

## Changes

- Pace options come from the system's travel pace config instead of a fixed list.
